(03) Why hand-built

Auto-export is fast. It’s also a trap.

WordPress still powers a huge share of the web because it’s flexible, familiar and yours to own — content, hosting and data all under your control. But that only pays off if it’s built well. A stretched marketplace theme with a dozen plugins can turn a beautiful Figma design into a slow, fragile site. We build lean — a custom theme or a disciplined page-builder setup — so you keep WordPress’s freedom without the bloat.

(05) FAQs

Figma to WordPress, answered.

How long does a build take?
A single landing page is usually 4–6 business days. A 5–8 page business site with a CMS typically takes 2–4 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.
Custom theme or page builder?
Both, depending on who maintains the site. We build lightweight custom themes (often with ACF and the block editor) when performance and control matter most, or Elementor/Gutenberg when your team wants to edit visually. We recommend the right fit for your case.
Will it look exactly like my Figma?
Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (hover states, mobile, empty states), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.
Is it easy for my team to edit?
Yes. We structure custom fields and editable blocks so your team can update text, images and posts safely — without breaking the design or touching code.
