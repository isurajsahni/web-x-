How much does a website cost?

It depends on what you need. A landing page and a custom web application are very different amounts of work, and a business website or ecommerce store sits somewhere in between. Whatever it is, you get a fixed price before we start. We don’t bill hourly and there are no surprise change orders. There’s a full breakdown in our guide to what a website costs.

What do you need from us to get started?

First, a conversation. We’ll want to know what the business does, who the site is for and what a good enquiry looks like for you. After that we need your brand assets, any copy you already have, and access to your domain and hosting. None of that has to be ready on day one. No copy? We write it.

How long does a website project take?

A landing page usually takes 3–5 business days. A standard business website takes 1–3 weeks, an ecommerce build 3–6 weeks, and a custom web application or ERP dashboard 6–12 weeks. Before we begin you’ll get a written milestone plan with fixed dates.

Where is Web{X} Studio based?

Our studio is in Model Town, Ludhiana, Punjab. We’re open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00. If you’re local, come and meet us. We also work with clients across Punjab, and remotely with teams in the rest of India, the UK, the US and the Gulf.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. A large share of our work is for founders and marketing teams in the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Singapore. Our working hours overlap with your morning, and we can invoice in INR, USD, GBP, EUR or AED.
