How the process works

  1. Share your Figma

    Send the file and a quick note on the campaign, forms, tools and any variants you want. We review it and come back with a fixed quote and timeline — usually within two business days.

  2. We build in Unbounce

    We rebuild your design section by section, responsive from the first breakpoint. You get a preview link to watch it come together.

  3. Forms, variants & tracking

    We connect forms and your CRM, set up A/B variants and Smart Traffic, add conversion tracking, and pressure-test every breakpoint and form state.

  4. Review & revisions

    You review against Figma; we refine until it matches. Two rounds of revisions are included on every build.

  5. Launch & handover

    We connect your domain or subdomain, run final QA and tracking checks, publish, and hand over the page with a walkthrough so your team can test and iterate.

Figma to Unbounce FAQs

How long does a build take?

A single landing page is usually 2–4 business days. A campaign set with variants and multiple pages typically takes 1–2 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.

Will it look exactly like my Figma?

Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (mobile, form states, thank-you pages), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.

Can you set up A/B tests?

Yes. We build variants for testing, wire up Smart Traffic where it helps, and structure the page so your team can duplicate and test without a developer.

Do you connect forms and tracking?

Absolutely — lead forms to your CRM or email tool, plus conversion tracking and analytics events, so every campaign click is measured from launch.
