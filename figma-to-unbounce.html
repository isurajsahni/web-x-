(05) FAQs

Unbounce questions

How long does Figma to Unbounce conversion typically take?
Usually 2-4 business days for one landing page. A campaign set with several pages and variants takes 1-2 weeks, depending on how many unique layouts, forms and integrations there are. We’ll give you a fixed timeline with the quote.
Will the Unbounce page look exactly like my Figma design?
Yes, at every breakpoint: spacing, type, colour and layout. Landing page files often leave out the mobile version, the form states or the thank-you page. If yours does, we’ll flag it and work out sensible defaults with you.
Can you set up A/B tests and variants?
Yes. We build the variants, turn on Smart Traffic where it helps, and set the page up so your marketing team can duplicate and test without a developer. You’ll also get a short Loom walkthrough.
How much does Figma to Unbounce cost?
It’s a fixed price for the whole job, with no hourly rate. The price depends on how many layouts and variants you need, which forms and integrations are involved, and how much A/B testing setup there is. We have a short scoping call, then send an itemised quote and a timeline. Once you approve it, the number stays put.
Do you connect forms, CRM and tracking?
Yes. Lead forms, your CRM and email tools, conversion tracking, and analytics or tag manager events. It’s all set up before launch, so you’re measuring from the very first click.
What do I need to send you to get started?
Start with the Figma link (view access) and the fonts or font licences it uses. Then final copy, or a note on what’s still placeholder, plus images and brand assets. We’ll also need access to your Unbounce account, your CRM and your ad tracking IDs. If mobile layouts, hover states or empty states aren’t designed yet, tell us and we’ll propose defaults for you to approve.
Is Unbounce the right platform for my Figma design?
For paid-campaign landing pages, usually yes, especially if your marketing team wants to launch and A/B test on their own. For a full website you’d be better off with Figma to Webflow or Figma to WordPress. For a store, Figma to Shopify. It’s worth reading our landing page best practices before you write the brief.
