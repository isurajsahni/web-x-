VCS Immigration

A visa and travel consultancy in Mullanpur Dakha, Ludhiana, and the school support brand it runs from the same office. We built one fast website that gives each offer its own clear path.

VCS helps students, families and professionals with visas for the UK, Canada, Australia, the USA and Europe, and Dakha Academy helps students who failed a class avoid losing a year. The site had to present both under one brand and make it easy to get in touch.

The challenge

  • Two offers, one brand. Visa consulting and open schooling serve different visitors, so each needed a clear path of its own without splitting the brand.
  • A high-trust decision. Visa applications involve money, documents and major life plans, so visitors need to feel confident before they get in touch.
  • Mostly mobile moments. Much of the audience needs to reach the office quickly, so calling and WhatsApp had to be easy to reach on a phone.

The details

A mobile menu that gets people in touch

A full-screen brand-blue panel grows out of the menu button, and numbered links slide in one after another. Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons sit at the bottom, so visitors can make contact in one tap from any page.

An endless destinations slider

A cloned set of cards sits on each side of the real ones. Once scrolling settles, the position jumps silently back into the original set, so the slider loops in both directions.

Click-to-play video

The "How it works" section shows a thumbnail with a play button. The video only downloads when someone presses play, so it adds nothing to the initial page load.

Interactions that respect the device

Service card descriptions appear on hover, and stay visible on touch screens that can't hover. The gallery is a draggable marquee on desktop and a simple swipeable row on phones. Testimonial cards open from the keyboard as well as the mouse.

Technical decisions

DecisionWhy
No framework or build stepA content-led marketing site doesn't need one. It's simple to host and easy to hand over.
WebP images and lazy loadingImages further down the page load only as needed.
Structured data on every pageSchema.org business details such as address, phone and hours help search engines understand the business.
Reduced-motion supportAnimations turn off for visitors who ask their device to reduce motion.
Natural text wrappingPage titles sit in a wider wrapper rather than using forced line breaks, so they reflow cleanly on any screen.

Problems solved

Clipped hover shadows. The slider's scroll area was cutting off the card shadow on hover. Extra padding, offset by matching negative margins, gave the shadow room without moving the layout.

A menu that opens from a moving button. The announcement bar shifts the header, so the menu button isn't always in the same place. The script now measures its position each time the menu opens, so the animation always starts from the button.

A cleaner mobile screen. The announcement bar took up too much of a phone screen, so it's now hidden on phones and the header sits at the top.
