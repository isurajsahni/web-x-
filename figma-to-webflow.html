(03) Why hand-built

Auto-export is fast. It’s also a trap.

Webflow gives you the freedom of a custom site with a CMS your team controls — but only if it’s built well. Plugins can convert a frame in minutes and leave you with markup no one can maintain. We build by hand for the same reason good tailors don’t use glue: it holds up.

A design is only as good as the site it becomes. We make sure nothing is lost in translation.

(05) FAQs

Figma to Webflow, answered.

How long does a Figma to Webflow build take?
A single landing page is usually 3-5 business days. A 5-8 page marketing site with a CMS typically takes 2-3 weeks, depending on the number of unique templates, animations and integrations. We give a fixed timeline with the quote.
Will the Webflow site look exactly like my Figma design?
Yes. We build pixel-perfect from your Figma file — spacing, type, colour and layout match your design at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (hover states, mobile, empty states) we flag them and design sensible defaults with you.
Do you set up the Webflow CMS?
Yes. We structure Collections, reference fields and dynamic lists so your team can add blog posts, case studies, team members or products without touching the design — and we hand over a short Loom walkthrough.
How much does Figma to Webflow cost?
Every Figma to Webflow build is quoted as one fixed number rather than an hourly rate. Cost is driven by page count, CMS structure, how much animation and interaction the design calls for, and any third-party integrations. After a short scoping call we send a fixed, itemised quote and a timeline, and that number does not change once you approve it.
Can you add custom animations and interactions?
Absolutely. We build scroll effects, hover states, page transitions and micro-interactions natively in Webflow, and drop in custom code where Webflow’s native tools stop — all kept performant and accessible.
