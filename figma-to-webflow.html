(03) Why hand-built

Why we don’t use auto-export.

Webflow lets you have a fully custom site and still give your team a CMS they control. That’s the appeal. But it depends on how well the project is built underneath. A plugin can convert a Figma frame in a few minutes, and the markup it leaves is hard for anyone to maintain. Building by hand is slower. It holds up better, though.

The test we use: could another Webflow developer open this project in a year and understand it?

Auto-export plugins

  • Tangled, duplicated classes
  • Breaks on real content
  • Shaky responsiveness
  • Bloated, slow markup
  • Hard to maintain

(05) FAQs

Figma to Webflow FAQs

How long does Figma to Webflow conversion typically take?
Plan on 3-5 business days for a single landing page. A 5-8 page marketing site with a CMS usually takes 2-3 weeks. It varies with the number of unique templates, animations and integrations, and you get a fixed timeline when we quote.
Will the Webflow site look exactly like my Figma design?
Yes. Spacing, type, colour and layout all match your file at every breakpoint. Where the Figma file is missing something, such as hover states, mobile layouts or empty states, we’ll flag it and agree a sensible default with you.
Do you set up the Webflow CMS?
We do. We set up Collections, reference fields and dynamic lists so your team can add blog posts, case studies, team members or products without touching the design. You’ll get a short Loom walkthrough as well.
How much does Figma to Webflow cost?
We quote a single fixed price for the build. Page count, CMS structure, the amount of animation and interaction, and any third-party integrations all affect it. After a short scoping call we send an itemised quote with a timeline, and the price doesn’t change once you’ve approved it.
Can you add custom animations and interactions?
Yes. Scroll effects, hover states, page transitions and small interactions, built natively in Webflow. When Webflow’s own tools run out, we write custom code. Either way we keep it fast and accessible.
What do I need to send you to get started?
A Figma link with view access and the fonts (or licences) used in the design. Final copy if you have it, or a note on what’s still placeholder. Images and brand assets. And the Webflow workspace or site you’d like it built in. If mobile, hover or empty states haven’t been designed yet, let us know and we’ll suggest defaults for you to approve.
Is Webflow the right platform for my Figma design?
Webflow works well for design-led marketing sites, content hubs and blogs, where your team wants to edit pages visually without a developer. If you’re mainly selling products, Figma to Shopify is usually a better fit. If you already run WordPress or rely on its plugins, go with Figma to WordPress. And for very motion-heavy sites, have a look at Figma to Framer.
