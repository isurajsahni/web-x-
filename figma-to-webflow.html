(03) Why hand-built

Auto-export is fast. It’s also a trap.

Webflow gives you the freedom of a custom site with a CMS your team controls — but only if it’s built well. Plugins can convert a frame in minutes and leave you with markup no one can maintain. We build by hand for the same reason good tailors don’t use glue: it holds up.

A design is only as good as the site it becomes. We make sure nothing is lost in translation.

(05) FAQs

Figma to Webflow, answered.

How long does a build take?
A single landing page is usually 3–5 business days. A 5–8 page marketing site with a CMS typically takes 2–3 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.
Will it look exactly like my Figma?
Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (hover states, mobile, empty states), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.
Do you set up the CMS?
Yes. We structure Collections, reference fields and dynamic lists so your team can publish blogs, case studies or products without touching the design.
Can you add custom animations?
Absolutely — scroll effects, hovers, page transitions and micro-interactions, built natively and kept fast and accessible. We drop in custom code only where Webflow’s native tools stop.
