How the process works

  1. Share your Figma

    Send the file and a quick note on scope, interactions and any integrations. We review it and come back with a fixed quote and timeline — usually within two business days.

  2. We build in Webflow

    We rebuild your design section by section, responsive from the first breakpoint, with a clean class system. You get a preview link to watch it come together.

  3. CMS, interactions & polish

    We wire up Collections, add the motion your design calls for, and pressure-test every breakpoint, hover and empty state.

  4. Review & revisions

    You review against Figma; we refine until it matches. Two rounds of revisions are included on every build.

  5. Launch & handover

    We connect your domain, run final QA and SEO checks, publish, and hand over the project with a walkthrough so your team can take it from here.

Why Webflow — and why hand-built

Webflow gives you the visual freedom of a custom site with the independence of a CMS your team actually controls. But that only holds if it’s built well. Auto-export tools and plugins can turn a Figma frame into Webflow in minutes — and leave you with tangled classes, broken responsiveness and markup no one can maintain. We build by hand for the same reason good tailors don’t use glue: it holds up.

A design is only as good as the site it becomes. We make sure nothing is lost in translation.

Figma to Webflow FAQs

How long does a build take?

A single landing page is usually 3–5 business days. A 5–8 page marketing site with a CMS typically takes 2–3 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.

Will it look exactly like my Figma?

Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (hover states, mobile, empty states), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.

Do you set up the CMS?

Yes. We structure Collections, reference fields and dynamic lists so your team can publish blogs, case studies or products without touching the design.

Can you add custom animations?

Absolutely — scroll effects, hovers, page transitions and micro-interactions, built natively and kept fast and accessible. We drop in custom code only where Webflow’s native tools stop.
