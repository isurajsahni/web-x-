2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through this website and through the enquiries that come from it — email, phone and WhatsApp.

When we deliver a project we often process data that belongs to our client rather than to us: content, user records, or data held in a system we are building. In that relationship we act as a processor, we act only on the client’s written instructions, and the terms of the relevant project agreement govern that data, not this policy.

3. What we collect

3.1 Information you give us

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, the services you are interested in, and your message. It also asks you to tick a box confirming you are happy for us to reply — we do not treat sending the form as consent on its own.

If you email us, call us, or message us on WhatsApp, we hold whatever you choose to include: your contact details, your company, your budget and timeline, and anything else you tell us about your project.

If you apply for a role, we hold your application, CV, portfolio links and the correspondence around it.

3.2 Information collected automatically

If you allow analytics cookies, we record technical information about your visit:

  • IP address (typically truncated or anonymised by our analytics providers before it reaches us)
  • Browser type and version, operating system, device type and screen size
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address — country and city level, not a street address
  • The pages you view, how long you stay, and the order you move through them
  • The page or search that referred you, and any campaign tags on the link you followed
  • Interactions on the page: clicks, scroll depth, and where your pointer moves

If you decline, none of this is collected beyond the basic server logs our host keeps for security.

3.3 What we do not collect

We do not ask for and do not want payment card numbers, government identity numbers, health data, or any other special-category data through this website. We do not buy contact lists, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Cookies and your choices

A cookie is a small file a site stores in your browser. We group ours into three categories:

  • Strictly necessary — keeping the site working and secure, and remembering the cookie choice you make below. These cannot be switched off, and they are the only ones that run before you decide.
  • Analytics — understanding which pages people read and where they lose interest, so we can improve them.
  • Behaviour and performance — heatmaps and session replays that show how the layout actually gets used.

We do not run advertising or retargeting cookies on this website.

Nothing optional loads until you choose. Analytics and session-recording tags stay blocked on your first visit until you accept them, and switching a category off later stops the corresponding tag on your next page view. You can change your mind whenever you like:

Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage on your own device — it is not sent to us and not shared with anyone. We ask again after 12 months. You can also block or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time, and most browsers offer a global privacy control our providers honour. Blocking optional cookies will not stop you using any part of this site.

6. Session recording and heatmaps

If you allow behaviour cookies, we use Microsoft Clarity to record how visitors move through the site. We are calling this out separately because it is more intrusive than ordinary page-count analytics, and you deserve to know it is available.

Clarity captures pointer movement, clicks, scrolling and page transitions, and replays them as an anonymous session. We use it to find layout that confuses people — a button nobody sees, a section everyone scrolls past.

Clarity automatically masks text entered into form fields, and we have not turned that masking off. We do not use it to identify individuals, and we do not attempt to match a recording to a named person. If you would rather not be recorded, leave the behaviour category switched off in your , or opt out through Microsoft.

8. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with:

  • The service providers listed in section 5, and only for the purpose described there.
  • Contractors and specialists who work with us on a project, under confidentiality obligations, and only where the work requires it.
  • Our accountants, insurers and legal advisers, where they need it to advise us.
  • A regulator, court or authority, where the law requires us to disclose it.
  • A buyer or successor, if the studio is ever sold or merged — with the same protections carried over.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

9. International transfers

We are based in India, and several of the providers above are based in the United States or operate globally. This means your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from a country other than your own.

Where data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, our providers rely on recognised safeguards — typically the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision. Copies of the relevant safeguards are available from those providers, and we can point you to them on request.

10. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become projects — up to 24 months, then deleted, so we have context if you come back to us.
  • Client project records and correspondence — for the life of the engagement and up to 7 years afterwards, to meet Indian accounting, tax and limitation requirements.
  • Job applications — up to 12 months after the decision, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Analytics data — retained according to each provider’s own schedule; Microsoft Clarity retains recordings for a limited rolling window rather than indefinitely.
  • Your cookie choice — 12 months, in your own browser, after which we ask again.
  • Server logs — a short rolling period for security and diagnostics.

When a retention period ends we delete the data or irreversibly anonymise it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access — ask for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data fixed.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete data we no longer have a reason to keep.
  • Restriction — ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.
  • Objection — object to processing we base on legitimate interests.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting what was lawful beforehand.
  • Nominate — under India’s DPDP Act, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Complain — to your data protection authority. In the EU or UK that is your national supervisory authority or the ICO; in India, the Data Protection Board once constituted.

We will never charge you for exercising these rights or treat you differently for having done so.

12. Exercising your rights

Email hello@thewebxstudio.com with the subject line “Privacy request” and tell us what you want. We may ask a question or two to confirm it is really you — we are not going to hand your data to someone else on request.

We aim to respond within 30 days. If a request is unusually complex we will tell you before that deadline and explain why we need longer.

13. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiries and project files is limited to the people who need it, protected by strong authentication, and reviewed when someone leaves. We choose providers with recognised security practices, and we keep the number of places your data sits deliberately small.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If a breach ever affects your personal data and is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

14. Children

This website is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Links to other sites

Our work, blog and social links point to sites we do not control — including clients’ live websites. Once you leave thewebxstudio.com, this policy no longer applies, and neither does your cookie choice here. We would encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you land on.

16. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes — a new tool, a new legal requirement, a new service. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects your rights we will do more than quietly edit the page: we will say so prominently, and where the law requires it, ask for your consent again.
