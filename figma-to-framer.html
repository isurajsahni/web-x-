(03) Why by hand

There’s a plugin for this. We don’t use it.

Framer gives you a CMS, hosting and some very good animation tools in one place, which is why design-led teams like it. But it only pays off if the project underneath is built properly. Export plugins will get a Figma frame into Framer in a few minutes. What you get back is a pile of absolutely positioned layers that falls apart on a phone, and animation that stutters. Rebuilding by hand takes longer, and we think it’s worth it.

People notice when an animation feels slightly off, even if they couldn’t tell you what’s wrong. That’s the part we’re fussy about.

Export plugins

  • Absolutely positioned layers
  • Breaks when real copy goes in
  • Wobbly on tablet and mobile
  • Motion that stutters
  • Hard for anyone to edit later

  • Tidy layers and components
  • Framer CMS, properly structured
  • Works at every screen size
  • Native motion, tested on phones
  • A project your team can run

(05) FAQs

Questions about Figma to Framer

How long does Figma to Framer conversion typically take?
A landing page is usually 2-4 business days. A 5-8 page site with a CMS is more like 1.5-3 weeks. It depends on how many unique templates there are and how much animation, plus any integrations. You’ll get a fixed timeline along with the quote.
Will the Framer site look exactly like my Figma design?
Yes. Spacing, type, colour and layout will match your file at every breakpoint. Figma files often leave a few things out (hover states, mobile, empty states). When we spot a gap we’ll tell you and suggest a sensible default, and you decide.
Do you set up the Framer CMS?
Yes. We set up the collections and dynamic pages so your team can add blog posts, case studies or projects without touching the design. There’s a short Loom walkthrough at handover too.
How much does Figma to Framer cost?
You get one fixed price for the whole build. We don’t charge hourly. What moves the price: page count, how much custom motion and interaction there is, how the CMS is structured, and any third-party integrations. After a short scoping call we send an itemised quote and a timeline. That number doesn’t change once you’ve approved it.
Can you build advanced animations and interactions?
Yes, and it’s a big part of why people pick Framer. Scroll effects, staggered reveals, page transitions, sticky and parallax sections, small hover details. We build them natively, keep them smooth and keep them accessible.
What do I need to send you to get started?
The Figma link (view access is enough), the fonts or font licences, your final copy or a note on which bits are still placeholder, images and brand assets, and the Framer project or workspace you want it built in. Haven’t designed mobile, hover or empty states yet? That’s fine. Tell us and we’ll propose defaults for you to approve.
Is Framer the right platform for my Figma design?
It’s a good fit for marketing and product sites with a lot of motion, where designers want to make edits themselves and you need to launch quickly. If you have heavier CMS needs, Figma to Webflow gives you more room. Selling products? Look at Figma to Shopify. Already on WordPress? Then Figma to WordPress makes more sense.
