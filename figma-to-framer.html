How the process works

  1. Share your Figma

    Send the file and a quick note on scope, the motion you want and any integrations. We review it and come back with a fixed quote and timeline — usually within two business days.

  2. We build in Framer

    We rebuild your design section by section, responsive from the first breakpoint, with a clean layer and component system. You get a preview link to watch it come together.

  3. CMS, motion & polish

    We wire up Collections, add the animations your design calls for, and pressure-test every breakpoint, hover and empty state on real devices.

  4. Review & revisions

    You review against Figma; we refine until it matches. Two rounds of revisions are included on every build.

  5. Launch & handover

    We connect your domain, run final QA and SEO checks, publish on Framer, and hand over the project with a walkthrough so your team can take it from here.

Why Framer — and why hand-built

Framer is built for teams who care about motion and want to ship without a heavy stack — a real CMS, fast hosting and animation tools that put agencies-grade interactions in reach. But that only pays off if it’s built well. Auto-export plugins can dump a Figma frame into Framer in minutes — and leave you with stacked absolute layers, broken responsiveness and motion that stutters. We build by hand so your site stays faithful, fast and smooth.

A design is only as good as the site it becomes — and motion is only as good as it feels. We make sure nothing is lost in translation.

Figma to Framer FAQs

How long does a build take?

A single landing page is usually 2–4 business days. A 5–8 page site with a CMS typically takes 1.5–3 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.

Will it look exactly like my Figma?

Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (hover states, mobile, empty states), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.

Do you set up the CMS?

Yes. We structure Collections and dynamic templates so your team can publish blogs, case studies or projects without touching the design.

Can you build advanced animations?

Absolutely — Framer is made for motion. Scroll effects, staggered reveals, parallax, page transitions and micro-interactions, built natively and kept smooth and accessible.
