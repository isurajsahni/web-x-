## The single most important thing: Google Business Profile (GBP)

Local-pack ranking ≈ **Relevance + Proximity + Prominence**, and GBP drives most of it. Pages alone will *not* rank you in the map pack without a verified GBP. Do this first (only you can — it needs your Google account + address verification):

1. **Create/claim:** business.google.com → add "Web{X} Studio" with your real Ludhiana address.
2. **Primary category:** `Website designer`. **Secondary categories:** `Website designer`, `Internet marketing service`, `Software company`, `Graphic designer`, `Marketing agency`. (Categories are a top-3 local ranking factor — pick the most specific primary.)
3. **NAP (Name, Address, Phone):** must be **identical** everywhere online — website footer, GBP, every directory. One format, forever. Use a local +91 number (a local landline/mobile beats a toll-free for local trust).
4. **Service area:** add Ludhiana + the Punjab cities you serve (Amritsar, Jalandhar, Patiala, Bathinda, Mohali/Chandigarh).
5. **Description:** natural, keyword-aware (e.g., "Web{X} is a website design and development studio in Ludhiana, Punjab, building fast, high-converting websites for businesses across India and worldwide."). No stuffing.
6. **Photos:** office, team, work samples, logo — 10+ real images. Profiles with photos get materially more clicks/calls.
7. **Products/Services:** list each service (Web Design, Website Development, Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, SEO, Logo Design) with short descriptions + price hints.
8. **Reviews (huge):** the #2 local factor after relevance/proximity.
   - Ask every happy client for a Google review with a direct review link.
   - Aim for a steady drip (2–4/month) not a burst.
   - **Reply to every review** (keyword-natural: "Thanks for trusting our Ludhiana web design team…").
9. **Google Posts:** post weekly (offers, new work, tips) — signals activity.
10. **Q&A:** seed 5–8 common questions and answer them yourself.
11. **Verify** (postcard/phone/video) and keep it 100% complete — completeness itself is a ranking factor.

> Also claim: **Bing Places**, **Apple Business Connect**, **Justdial**, **Sulekha**, **IndiaMART** (see citations below).
