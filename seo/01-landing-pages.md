# Web{X} — Landing Page Architecture (150+ pages)

> Companion to `00-master-strategy.md`. Every URL is root-flat, lowercase, kebab-case, no `.html` (matches your live convention).
> Meta titles ≤ ~60 chars; descriptions 140–155 chars. `✅` = already live.
> **Schema per section** is stated once (all pages also get BreadcrumbList + the site-wide Organization/WebSite `@id` reference).
> **Don't ship all at once** — see build sequencing in the master strategy. Location/industry pages require real differentiation (case study + locale FAQs) before publishing.

Legend — Intent: **T**=Transactional · **C**=Commercial-investigation · **I**=Informational.
