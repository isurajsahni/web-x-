How much does a website cost in Punjab?

From Ludhiana’s exporters to Mohali’s startups and Amritsar’s retailers, everyone asks the same first question. Here are real 2026 price ranges in INR, what actually drives the number, and how to make sure you pay for outcomes - not hours.

Punjab’s business landscape is unusually varied - textile and cycle giants in Ludhiana, sports-goods and hand-tool exporters in Jalandhar, tourism and food brands in Amritsar, and a fast-growing IT and startup scene in Mohali. So “how much does a website cost in Punjab” has no single answer. But it does have honest ranges, and a clear logic behind them.

At Web{X} Studio, a web development agency in Ludhiana, we’ve quoted projects for businesses across the state and abroad. This guide lays out what websites genuinely cost in Punjab in 2026 and the seven things that decide where your project lands.

The short answer

In 2026, a professional small-business website in Punjab usually runs ₹40,000-₹2,00,000. Ecommerce and custom builds run ₹2,50,000-₹15,00,000+. A single high-converting landing page can start around ₹40,000. Where you land depends on design, scope, integrations and performance.

What actually drives the price

If two Punjab studios quote the same project at ₹80,000 and ₹4,00,000, one is usually mis-reading the brief. Seven factors explain almost every gap:

  • Custom design vs templates - the single biggest swing. Bespoke, brand-led design costs more and looks nothing like your competitor’s theme.
  • Number of unique page templates - fifty products share one template; five distinct pages are five designs.
  • Integrations - payments, WhatsApp, CRM, inventory, multi-language and export enquiry flows are real engineering.
  • Motion and interaction - the polish on premium sites is deliberate build time, worth it for brands selling on craft.
  • Performance & SEO - a fast site that passes Core Web Vitals is built differently from one that “just works.”
  • Content - if the studio writes copy or art-directs a shoot, that’s scope. Unready content is the top cause of overruns.
  • Support after launch - hosting, edits, security and SEO are either a retainer or a surprise.

Does the city inside Punjab change the price?

Slightly, but less than you’d think. Rates in Ludhiana, Amritsar, Jalandhar, Patiala and Bathinda are broadly similar; Mohali/Chandigarh studios sometimes price a little higher because of the IT-hub cost base. The far bigger variable is who you hire - a template shop and a proper design studio in the same city can differ 5x. Hire on portfolio and engineering standards, not location. If you sell internationally, a Punjab studio can deliver export-grade quality at 40-70% less than a US or UK agency.

The real question isn’t “how cheap can this be” - it’s “what is a website that wins customers worth to my business?”

How to avoid overpaying (or underbuying)

  • Lead with the goal, not the budget. “We want 30% more export enquiries” gets a sharper proposal than “make a website.”
  • Share a rough sitemap. A bullet list of pages turns a vague quote into a precise one.
  • Get content moving early. It’s the most common cause of delay and cost creep.
  • Confirm ownership and support in writing. Domain, hosting, files and post-launch edits should all be defined.
  • Beware the suspiciously cheap. A ₹8,000 “complete website” is a template with your logo - it won’t rank or scale.

So, what should you budget?

If your website is a real sales channel - where a customer or overseas buyer decides whether to trust you - treat it as an investment with a return. For most growing Punjab businesses in 2026, a serious, fast, SEO-ready website lands between ₹1,00,000 and ₹5,00,000. Spend below and you’re usually buying a template; spend above and you’re buying bespoke design, deep integrations or a full platform.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a basic website cost in Punjab?
A basic professional website of 3-5 pages in Punjab typically costs ₹40,000-₹1,20,000 from a studio in 2026. Template DIY sites can be cheaper but rarely rank or scale. A single high-converting landing page starts around ₹40,000.
Is a website cheaper in Punjab than in Delhi or Mumbai?
Often yes. Studios in Punjab cities like Ludhiana, Amritsar and Jalandhar usually have lower operating costs than metro agencies, so you can get the same quality build for 20-40% less. What matters is the studio’s portfolio and engineering standards, not the pin code.
How much does an ecommerce website cost in Punjab?
An ecommerce store in Punjab typically costs ₹2,50,000-₹8,00,000 depending on catalogue size, custom design, payment and shipping integrations, and whether it is built on Shopify or a custom stack.
Are there ongoing costs after the website is built?
Yes. Budget for domain (₹800-₹1,500/yr), hosting (₹3,000-₹15,000/yr or a Webflow/Shopify plan), and optional maintenance or SEO retainers. Clarify what is included with your studio before signing.
