(07) FAQs

Web design in Punjab, answered.

Which is the best web design company in Punjab?
The best one for you is the company whose live websites are fast on a phone, who designs and develops in-house, includes SEO in the build, gives a fixed written quote and puts your domain and code in your name. Ask every studio you shortlist, including us, to prove each of those before you pay.
Do you work with businesses outside Ludhiana?
Yes. We work with businesses across Punjab, including Amritsar, Jalandhar, Mohali, Patiala, Bathinda, Hoshiarpur, Pathankot, Moga and Phagwara, as well as Chandigarh. Most projects run over video calls, WhatsApp and a live preview link, and you are welcome to visit our studio in Model Town, Ludhiana.
How much does website designing cost in Punjab?
It depends on the number of pages, how custom the design is, and whether you need ecommerce or integrations. We send a fixed, itemised quote within two business days of a short call, and that number does not change once you approve it. Our website cost guide for Punjab explains what drives the price.
Do I need to visit your office to get a website made?
No. Every stage can be done remotely: a kickoff call, screen-shared design reviews, a live preview link to check on your phone, and a recorded handover. If you would rather meet, the studio is in Model Town, Ludhiana, open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00.
How long does it take to design a website?
A landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks, a business website 3–6 weeks and an ecommerce store 6–10 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly your content, photos and feedback arrive. You get a stage-by-stage timeline with your quote.
Will my website rank for searches in my own city?
Every site we build is fast, mobile-first and set up with titles, structured data and a sitemap. For local searches we also help set up and optimise your Google Business Profile and write pages around what customers in your city actually search for. Rankings take time and are never guaranteed, but these are the foundations they depend on.
What is the difference between a web design company and a web development company?
Web design decides how the site looks and how people use it; web development builds it in code and connects it to forms, payments and a CMS. Some companies only do one. We do both in-house, so there is no handoff between a designer and a separate developer.
