(01) What it is

A landing page is not a smaller website.

A website serves many audiences across many pages. A landing page serves one campaign, one audience and one action. Strip the competing navigation, and every element left on the page has to earn its place by moving the visitor toward that single goal — which is why landing pages convert so much better than pointing paid traffic at a homepage.

We design and build them end to end: message hierarchy, responsive design, form and proof placement, page speed, and the analytics needed to tell whether it worked. See the full service range, or read landing page vs website if you are deciding which you need.

(02) CRO

Conversion rate optimisation, not decoration.

CRO means improving the share of visitors who take the action you want. On a landing page that is structural, not cosmetic: what the headline promises, how far the proof sits from the ask, how many fields the form demands, and how fast the page paints on a phone on mobile data.

  • Message match. The page says what the ad promised, in the same words.
  • One action. No competing nav, no second CTA pulling in another direction.
  • Proof where the doubt is. Testimonials and logos next to the ask, not buried.
  • Form friction. Every extra field costs conversions; we ask only what you will use.
  • Speed. Core Web Vitals treated as a conversion input, because it is one.
  • Measurement. Event tracking set up so the next version is informed, not guessed.

(03) FAQs

Landing pages, answered.

What is landing page design?
Landing page design is building a single, focused page aimed at one audience and one action — a signup, a demo request, a purchase. Unlike a homepage, it has no competing navigation, so every element on it exists to move the visitor toward that one goal.
How is a landing page different from a website?
A website serves many audiences and many goals across multiple pages. A landing page serves one campaign, one audience and one conversion. That focus is why landing pages typically convert far better than sending paid traffic to a homepage.
What is CRO and do you include it?
CRO is conversion rate optimisation — improving the share of visitors who take the action you want. It is built into every landing page we ship: message hierarchy, form design, proof placement and page speed, then measurement after launch so the page keeps improving.
How long does a landing page take to build?
A focused landing page is usually 2-3 weeks from brief to live, including design, build, responsive testing and analytics setup. Turnaround depends on how ready your copy and brand assets are.
Are the pages A/B testing ready?
Yes. Pages are built so variants can be swapped without a redevelopment cycle, and we set up the analytics and event tracking needed to tell which variant actually won.
Which platforms do you build landing pages on?
Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, Framer, Unbounce, or fully custom — chosen for how your team needs to edit and test the page, not for our convenience.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes. We work with founders and marketing teams across the UK, the US, the Gulf, Australia and Canada as well as India, running projects remotely with async updates and calls that fit your hours.
