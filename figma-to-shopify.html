How the process works

  1. Share your Figma

    Send the file and a quick note on scope, catalogue size, apps and integrations. We review it and come back with a fixed quote and timeline — usually within two business days.

  2. We build your theme

    We rebuild your design section by section in a clean Shopify theme, responsive from the first breakpoint. You get a preview store to watch it come together.

  3. Products, apps & polish

    We set up templates, connect apps and payments, add the motion your design calls for, and pressure-test every breakpoint, variant and empty state.

  4. Review & revisions

    You review against Figma; we refine until it matches. Two rounds of revisions are included on every build.

  5. Launch & handover

    We connect your domain, run final QA, speed and SEO checks, publish, and hand over the store with a walkthrough so your team can take it from here.

Why Shopify — and why hand-built

Shopify gives you a battle-tested checkout, a huge app ecosystem and the freedom to run your store without babysitting servers. But a beautiful design only converts if the theme is built well. Auto-export tools and bloated premium themes can get a Figma frame online quickly — and leave you with slow pages, tangled Liquid and sections no one can safely edit. We build by hand so your store stays fast, faithful and easy to run as you scale.

A store design is only as good as the checkout it becomes. We make sure nothing is lost between the mockup and the sale.

Figma to Shopify FAQs

How long does a build take?

A single-page storefront is usually 4–6 business days. A full custom theme with product, collection and cart templates typically takes 2–4 weeks. We commit to a fixed timeline with your quote.

Will it look exactly like my Figma?

Yes — pixel-perfect at every breakpoint. Where Figma leaves gaps (product pages, cart, empty states, mobile), we flag them and design sensible defaults with you rather than guessing.

Do you build editable sections?

Yes. We build with Online Store 2.0 sections and blocks so your team can rearrange, duplicate and merchandise content in the theme editor without touching code.

Can you integrate apps and subscriptions?

Absolutely — reviews, upsells, subscriptions, payments and shipping, wired in and kept fast. We add custom Liquid only where an app stops.
