(03) Why custom

Why we skip the premium theme.

Shopify gets a lot right. The checkout is well tested, there’s an app for nearly everything, and you never have to think about servers. The theme is where stores run into trouble. Auto-export tools and heavy premium themes can get a design online quickly, but you end up with slow pages, messy Liquid and sections your team is nervous to edit. We’d rather build it by hand and keep it easy to run as the store grows.

What we check before launch: does the product page load quickly on a phone, and is it obvious how to get to checkout?

(05) FAQs

Shopify build FAQs

How long does Figma to Shopify conversion typically take?
A single-page storefront usually takes 4-6 business days. A full custom theme, with product, collection and cart templates, is more like 2-4 weeks. The spread comes down to how many unique sections you need and how many apps and integrations are involved. The quote comes with a fixed timeline.
Will the Shopify store look exactly like my Figma design?
It will. We match spacing, type, colour and layout to your file at every breakpoint. Store designs often skip a few screens, like the cart, empty states or the mobile product page. If yours does, we’ll point it out and agree sensible defaults with you.
Do you build editable Online Store 2.0 sections?
Yes. The whole theme is built from sections and blocks, so your team can rearrange, duplicate and edit content in Shopify’s theme editor without writing code. We’ll also send a short Loom walkthrough.
How much does Figma to Shopify cost?
One fixed price for the whole build. We don’t work hourly. The things that affect it most are the number of unique sections and templates, how much custom theme work the design needs, and which apps and integrations the store uses. After a short scoping call we send an itemised quote and a timeline, and the price is locked once you approve it.
Can you integrate apps, payments and subscriptions?
Yes. Payments, shipping, reviews, upsells, subscriptions and marketing apps. Where an app can’t do what you need, we write custom Liquid or code to fill the gap. We also keep an eye on speed, since every app adds some weight to the store.
What do I need to send you to get started?
Here’s the list: the Figma link with view access, your fonts (or font licences), final copy or a note on what’s still placeholder, images and brand assets, collaborator access to your Shopify store, and an export of your product data. If some states aren’t designed yet, like mobile, hover or empty states, just say so. We’ll suggest defaults for you to approve.
Is Shopify the right platform for my Figma design?
If selling is the main job, yes. Shopify handles checkout, payments, inventory and shipping out of the box. For a marketing site with no store, Figma to Webflow or Figma to WordPress is simpler. And for a landing page behind a paid campaign, try Figma to Unbounce.
