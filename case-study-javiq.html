The brief

JAVIQ's store ran on a hosted platform, which limited both its design and what customers could do on it. The brief came with one constraint that shaped the whole build: everything had to work on free plugins, with no monthly cost for Elementor Pro. Features that normally need Pro, such as the mega menu, popups and cart and checkout design, were built in a custom child theme instead.

What we built

Storefront

  • A home page based on the client's Figma file, with separate hero banners for desktop and phones
  • A shop page with a toolbar and "Shop by range" filters
  • Rich product pages with photo stories, ingredients and how-to-use steps
  • A custom desktop mega menu and a phone header with a search bar
  • Customer video testimonials and a reviews row using real customer testimonials

Shopping and checkout

  • One product card design across the site, with a Bestseller label and wishlist heart
  • Add to Cart that changes to Buy Now on cards, and size selector pills for products with variations
  • A redesigned cart and checkout, keeping WooCommerce's own block checkout underneath
  • Forgiving product search that handles typos and joined words such as "hairoil"

Payments, accounts and marketing

  • Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking) and Cash on Delivery, with free delivery on online payments and a flat ₹68 charge on Cash on Delivery that updates live
  • A custom My Account dashboard, wishlist and order tracking pages, and a floating WhatsApp button
  • A welcome offer popup, an exit popup that collects phone numbers, and a 5-product gift box page
  • SEO titles and descriptions for pages, products and categories, product rich-result data and an XML sitemap

How we kept it maintainable

The client stays in control

Content lives in Elementor and WooCommerce, not in code. Tagging a product best-seller updates every section that uses that tag.

Every change is repeatable and reversible

Site changes were made by numbered scripts that back up what they touch first.

Claims stay honest

Product copy leaves out anything that could not be verified, offer prices were checked against the catalogue, and only real customer reviews are shown.

How it was tested

  • Responsive: 26 pages at 6 screen widths, from 320 to 1440 px, in 156 checks for sideways scrolling, script errors, failed files, headings and alt text
  • Links: 52 pages crawled, with no broken links or redirect chains
  • Customer journeys: 18 checks from cart to coupon, Cash on Delivery order and tracking
  • Accounts: 13 checks covering wishlist, search, registration, dashboard, logout and the contact form
  • Manual: a real Razorpay cancel-and-retry on the hosted site, and layout checks on desktop, tablet and phone
