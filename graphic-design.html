What is the difference between graphic design and branding?

Branding is the decision about how your business should come across — its personality, its positioning and the visual identity that expresses it. Graphic design is the craft of making things with that identity: the logo itself, the packaging, the brochure, the social post.

Most businesses need both, in that order: an identity first, so every piece of graphic design after it has rules to follow. If it is the website you need, that is our web design service — and the same team does both.

How long does a branding project take?

It depends on scope. A logo refresh is quicker than a full identity with guidelines, packaging and templates, and the number of feedback rounds matters as much as the design work. You get a written timeline with review dates alongside the fixed quote, before any work begins.

The most common delay is not design — it is waiting on feedback, so nominating one decision-maker for approvals is the single fastest thing you can do.

What files will we receive?

Your logo in every version you need — full colour, one colour, reversed and icon-only — as vector files for print and PNG and SVG files for the web, plus colour codes, font details and any templates in the scope.

Everything is organised, clearly named and yours to keep, with brand guidelines that explain how to use it.

Can you refresh our existing brand instead of starting over?

Yes. Often the right answer is to keep what customers already recognise and fix what is holding it back — a cleaner logo, a tighter colour palette, consistent type — rather than throwing away years of recognition.

After looking at what you have, we will tell you honestly whether it needs a refresh or a rebuild.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted as a fixed price agreed before any work begins — no hourly billing and no surprise change orders. The quote lists exactly what you get — concepts, revision rounds, files and any print or packaging artwork — with anything that recurs (font licences, stock images) listed separately so you can see it.

Scope is what moves the number, so if your budget is fixed, say so on the first call and we will scope to it rather than quote past it.

What do you need from us to get started?

A conversation first: what the business does, who it sells to and how you want people to feel about it. After that, any existing logo files or brand material, examples you like, and where the brand will be used most.

None of it is a blocker. If there are no brand files we start fresh. If you are not sure what you like, we will show you options.
