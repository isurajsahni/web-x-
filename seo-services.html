Is Your Website
Invisible on Google?

Most buyers start with a search. If you aren’t on the first page for what you sell, those buyers go to a competitor, and it often isn’t a better business, just one that’s easier to find.

You Don’t Show Up for What You Sell

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes such as indexing problems, broken redirects and slow pages can show results within weeks of Google recrawling the site. Ranking for competitive terms usually takes several months of steady work, and the more established your competitors are, the longer it takes.

We’ll tell you after the audit which wins are close and which will take longer, so you can judge progress against a realistic plan. Why speed matters so much is covered in our Core Web Vitals guide.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and no honest SEO agency can. Google decides rankings, and its own guidance warns against anyone who promises a guaranteed position. What we can commit to is the work: a clear monthly plan, fixes shipped on schedule, and reporting that shows whether it’s moving leads.

How much do your SEO services cost?

A one-off audit and fix project is quoted as a single fixed price, and an ongoing retainer as a fixed monthly price. Both are agreed before any work begins, with no hourly billing. What moves the number is the size of the site, how competitive your market is and how much content the plan needs.

If your budget is fixed, say so on the first call and we’ll scope to it. For wider context, our guide to hiring an SEO agency compares typical agency prices.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with businesses in India, the UK, the US, the Gulf, Canada and Australia, running projects remotely with written updates and calls that fit your hours. International SEO covers the right hreflang, spelling and currency for each market.

Closer to home, we also run local SEO for businesses across Ludhiana and Punjab.

What’s included in a monthly SEO retainer?

Ongoing technical monitoring and fixes, new and improved pages from the content plan, local SEO work if you serve specific areas, authority building, and a monthly report with the plan for the month ahead. The exact mix is set by the audit and written into the scope.

Do I need a new website to improve my SEO?

Usually not. Most sites can be fixed where they are, and we’ll only suggest a rebuild if the platform is genuinely holding you back. If it is, our web development team handles the rebuild with a full redirect map, so you keep the rankings you’ve already earned.
