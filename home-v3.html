How long does a project take?

A landing page is usually 3–5 business days. A standard business website takes 1–3 weeks, and an ecommerce store or web app 3–8 weeks, depending on scope.

How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts around ₹40,000. A business website typically runs ₹40,000–₹2,00,000, with ecommerce and custom builds higher. Every project is quoted as a fixed price before we start.

Do you handle both design and development?

Yes. Design, build, and the SEO that keeps it found all happen in the same team, so nothing gets lost in a handover.

Which platforms do you build on?

Webflow, WordPress, Shopify, Framer, or fully custom — chosen for how your team needs to edit the site, not for our convenience.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with founders and marketing teams across the UK, the US, the Gulf, Australia and Canada as well as India, with hours that overlap your morning.

Can you work with our existing team?

Often that is the arrangement — we slot in alongside an in-house marketer or developer and take the design and build load.

Can you support us after launch?

Yes. Monthly support covers changes, new pages, performance and SEO once the site is live.
